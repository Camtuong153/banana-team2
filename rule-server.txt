# [[ SERVER RULE ]]

**1. Respect Everyone**
Treat all members with courtesy and respect. Harassment, hate speech...

**2. No Spam or Self-Promotion**
Avoid spamming text, emojis, capital letters...

**3. Keep Content Safe & Appropriate (NSFW/SFW)**
Do not share Explicit, NSFW, gore...

**4. Use the Right Channels**
Ensure your text and media stay in the designated channels...

**5. Follow Discord Terms of Service**
All members must adhere to Discord's Terms of Service...

---

### Enforcement & Staff Policy
* **First Offense:** Verbal Warning / Message Deletion
* **Second Offense:** Temporary Mute / Timeout
* **Severe/Repeated Offenses:** Kick or Permanent Ban
