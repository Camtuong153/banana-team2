[[ SERVER RULE ]]
​1. Respect Everyone
Treat all members with courtesy and respect. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, bullying, and personal attacks will not be tolerated under any circumstances.
​2. No Spam or Self-Promotion
Avoid spamming text, emojis, capital letters, or mass tags. Unsolicited direct messages (DMs) to members and unauthorized self-promotion, advertising, or referral links are strictly prohibited.
​3. Keep Content Safe & Appropriate (NSFW/SFW)
Do not share Explicit, NSFW, gore, or sexually suggestive content anywhere in this server. Keep all profile pictures, statuses, and usernames appropriate.
​4. Use the Right Channels
Ensure your text and media stay in the designated channels (e.g., keep bot commands in #bot-commands and media in #media). Check channel topic descriptions before posting.
​5. Follow Discord Terms of Service
All members must adhere to Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
​Enforcement & Staff Policy
​First Offense: Verbal Warning / Message Deletion
​Second Offense: Temporary Mute / Timeout
​Severe/Repeated Offenses: Kick or Permanent Ban
​Server Staff reserve the right to act on any behavior harmful to the community, even if not explicitly listed above.
